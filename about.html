About

The Skunk Ape Report is a fan-made site tracking what players are discovering in GTA 6: tips, glitches, cheat code status, easter eggs, and the gloriously dumb fun in between. It is named after Leonida's own swamp-dwelling cryptid, which tells you most of what you need to know about our editorial priorities.

How we work

The site is updated multiple times a day. Nothing runs here unless it is credited to its source, and gameplay finds are listed only once they are reported by reliable outlets or linked to original community posts. Cheat codes get a stricter bar: a code appears here only after at least two reliable sources confirm it works, because the internet is already full of fabricated code lists and we would rather be slow than wrong.

Community finds

Readers can submit their own discoveries through the submission sheet on the home page. Every submission is reviewed by a strict moderator before publishing. Approved finds run with credit to the submitter's handle; anything inappropriate, off topic, or unverifiable simply never runs.

The monthly roundup

Once a month we send the best new finds in one email. Sign up from the home page, or email "sign me up" to skunkapereport@proton.me.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

The Skunk Ape Report ("we", "us") is a fan-made website at skunkapereport.com. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. The short version: we collect very little, and nothing without a reason.

Information you give us

  • Newsletter signups. If you email us to join the monthly roundup, we keep your email address solely to send that roundup. We do not sell, rent, or share it. You can unsubscribe anytime by emailing skunkapereport@proton.me with "unsubscribe", and we will delete your address.
  • Community submissions. If you submit a find, we receive the text you send: the find, any proof link, and the handle you choose. Approved submissions are published with that handle. We may edit submissions for clarity and style. Do not include personal information in a submission.

Information collected automatically

  • Local storage. The site stores small values in your own browser (for example, that you dismissed the signup popup) so it does not show you the same thing twice. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us.
  • Analytics. If we use an analytics service (such as Google Analytics), it collects standard usage data: pages visited, approximate location, device and browser type. We use this only to understand what readers find useful.
  • Hosting logs. Our hosting providers may keep standard server logs (IP address, pages requested) for security and operations.

Advertising and cookies

We may display advertising served by third parties such as Google AdSense. Advertising partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads, including ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies is described at policies.google.com/technologies/ads, and you can opt out of personalized advertising at adssettings.google.com. Where required by law, you will be asked for consent before advertising cookies are set.

Third-party links

The site links to outside sources (news outlets, Reddit, YouTube) for credit and proof. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices.

Children

This site covers a video game rated for mature audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Data retention and your rights

We keep newsletter emails until you unsubscribe and submissions for as long as the site operates. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information. To exercise them, email us and we will respond promptly.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
